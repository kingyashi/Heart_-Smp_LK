How it works

🎲 Get a heart

Join for the first time and you receive a random heart with its own powers. Every heart also gives you +1 extra heart of health.

⚔️ Steal hearts

Kill another player and you take their heart(s) too. Stack powers - but everyone will be hunting you.

🔨 Only 3 maces

Just three maces exist on the whole server. Admins can always see who holds them.

🐉 The Ender Heart can't be rolled - it only goes to the player who kills the Ender Dragon.

The 10 Hearts

Check yours in-game with /heart info - see who has what with /hearts.
